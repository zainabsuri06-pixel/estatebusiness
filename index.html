About Estate Business

A Legacy of Trust Since 2000

Estate Business is a family-led real estate consultancy established in 2000 by Mr. Rizwan Rana, who laid the foundation of the business with a vision of providing reliable, transparent and professional real estate services.

Built on the values of trust, integrity and long-term relationships, Estate Business has continued to grow while maintaining its family-oriented approach and commitment to clients.

Today, the legacy continues under the leadership of Mr. Farhan Rana, Managing Director, bringing a fresh vision, modern strategies and a forward-thinking approach to the industry.

Combining years of experience with a new generation’s perspective, Estate Business remains committed to helping clients make confident property decisions through professional guidance, market knowledge and personalized service.

From property sales and purchases to rentals and investment opportunities, our goal remains the same: building lasting relationships through trust and delivering value with every transaction.
